# OmniSign — 2-Minute Demo Video Script
### Submission Video for iQOO Hackathon 2026 Finale & Evaluation

**Total Duration:** 2 minutes (120 seconds)  
**Setting:** A single smartphone running OmniSign in an apartment setting receiving an incoming Zomato delivery call.

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] The Hook & The Everyday Crisis
**Visual:** A deaf resident orders food on his phone. Suddenly, his phone rings with an incoming call from "Ramesh Kumar — Zomato Partner". The resident answers, but cannot speak. The rider says: *"Bhaiya, which tower and flat?"* The line is silent. Rider looks frustrated.  
**Presenter (Voiceover):**  
> *"In India, over 2.7 crore people are non-vocal, mute, or deaf. Every day, they order food on Zomato, groceries on Blinkit, or cabs on Uber. But when the delivery rider reaches the gate and calls: 'Bhaiya, which building and flat?', communication freezes. Delivery riders on two-wheelers cannot read chat messages in traffic. Without a voice, orders get cancelled, rides drop, and independence is lost.*  
> *Meet OmniSign — a real-time mobile call bridge that turns hand signs directly into spoken voice on live phone calls."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:55] Live Call Answer & Sign-to-Voice (Resident &rarr; Rider)
**Visual:** The incoming phone call rings with authentic dual-tone audio. The resident taps **"Answer Call"**. The phone's front camera activates with MediaPipe skeleton tracking.  
**Presenter:**  
> *"OmniSign runs entirely on a single smartphone. The rider asks directions. The resident simply signs into the front camera: he points left for GATE_LEFT and flashes two fingers for 2ND FLOOR.*  
> *OmniSign's 72-D invariant kinematic engine detects the gesture in 36 microseconds. Instantly, the phone's voice bridge speaks aloud into the active call."*  
**Phone Speaker (Natural Voice Aloud):**  
> *"Bhaiya, please take a left turn at the main society gate. 2nd floor, flat 204. Please leave the package at the door."*

---

### [0:55 – 1:25] Rider Voice & Live Subtitles (Rider &rarr; Resident)
**Visual:** The delivery partner on the motorcycle hears the voice loud and clear through his helmet earphones.  
**Rider speaks into his phone:** *"Got it bhaiya! Coming up to the 2nd floor now."*  
**Visual:** On the resident's screen, the animated audio waveform reacts, and real-time captions instantly appear:  
> *"Ramesh (Voice): Got it bhaiya! Coming up to the 2nd floor now."*  
**Presenter:**  
> *"Continuous on-device ASR transcribes the rider's response directly onto the screen. Two-way, instantaneous communication on a live call with zero external devices."*

---

### [1:25 – 1:50] Under the Hood (Technical Innovation)
**Visual:** Screen cuts to the live telemetry HUD: 72-D feature vector, 36.0 µs latency, 27,775 FPS throughput, and 83/83 unit tests passing.  
**Presenter:**  
> *"Under the hood: MediaPipe 21-landmark tracking on Snapdragon 8 Elite NPU, 100% scale and translation invariant Euclidean geometry, and deterministic trilingual dialogue synthesis in English, Hindi, and Telugu.*  
> *Zero cloud delay, zero data leakage, 100% offline."*

---

### [1:50 – 2:00] Closing & Impact
**Visual:** The resident opens the apartment door and picks up his food parcel with a smile. Gives a quick thumbs up.  
**Presenter:**  
> *"OmniSign restores daily independence to 2.7 crore non-vocal citizens across every delivery, ride, and phone call in India. Thank you!"*
